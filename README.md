# Nexus AI – Executive Report Compiler

live link: https://ml-project-2-cmah.onrender.com

## Project Overview

**Nexus AI – Executive Report Compiler** is an advanced Business Intelligence and Analytics platform developed for Sales Forecasting and Inventory Optimization. The system transforms raw sales and inventory datasets into interactive executive reports, dashboards, and visual analytics that support data-driven decision-making.

The application integrates data analysis, visualization, reporting, and forecasting capabilities into a unified platform, enabling organizations to monitor business performance, identify trends, optimize inventory, and generate professional reports.

---

# Project Objectives

* Automate executive report generation.
* Analyze sales performance across products and regions.
* Monitor revenue trends and business growth.
* Identify anomalies in operational data.
* Provide inventory and sales insights.
* Generate professional management reports.
* Support business forecasting and strategic planning.
